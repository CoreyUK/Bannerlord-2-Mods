Strategic Campaign AI - v0.3.1
==============================

Requires Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.4.8.


Changes
-------
- Armies now commit to a siege rather than switching between nearby targets.
- Kingdoms hold a defensive footing steadily instead of alternating between
  defending and attacking.
- Army roles are held for a minimum period, so armies stop changing course.
- Siege judgement counts only a rival kingdom's relieving force, not troops
  already inside the walls.
- A failed siege is remembered per kingdom, so an army does not turn straight
  back to the same target.
- Threat assessment is now consistent from one check to the next.
- Strategic memory is tidied for armies that no longer exist.

Added settings: SiegeAbandonConfirmationHours, RoleMinimumHoldHours,
UrgentDefenseLatchHours.
Strategic Campaign AI - unreleased
=================================

Requires Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.4.8.


Fixed
-----
- Armies no longer bounce between two nearby castles, starting a siege, lifting
  it, and starting the other. The "this siege is hopeless" verdict could only
  ever apply to the siege actually in progress, so lifting one deleted the
  penalty and the abandoned castle looked worth taking again straight away. The
  verdict is now reached once an hour and has to hold for several hours before
  an army acts on it.
- Troops sheltering inside a besieged settlement no longer count as a force
  coming to relieve it. Two or three lords holed up in a castle used to make a
  siege look hopeless the moment it began.
- Giving up a siege, or losing the besieging army, now records a failed target,
  so the AI does not turn straight back around. That cooldown existed but was
  unreachable unless direct move orders were enabled, and it is now recorded per
  faction rather than globally.
- Armies no longer flip between defending a fief and besieging one every few
  hours. Roles are kept for a minimum time instead of being rebuilt from a
  strength ranking on every planning tick, and the kingdom's defensive footing
  now needs a quiet spell before it lifts rather than tracking a bare threat
  count.
- Defending an objective now carries the same commitment bonus as attacking one,
  so an army stops abandoning a fief the instant its arrival ends the siege or
  raid that drew it there.
- Nearby-strength results are no longer shared between callers asking about
  different radii. Six callers share these caches across sweeps of 45 to 95
  units, and whichever asked first each hour decided the answer everyone got,
  which made both siege and threat assessment change their minds hour to hour
  with nothing having moved.
- Low supplies no longer penalise a siege already under way, only the decision
  to start a new one. A besieging army's food routinely dips below three days.
- An army's objective is no longer forgotten when the game briefly clears its
  destination, which used to drop the commitment bonus at the exact moment it
  was needed.
- Objectives belonging to armies that no longer exist are cleared from the save.
  They were never pruned, and each one held a settlement under the duplicate-
  target penalty for every army still in the field.


Added
-----
- settings.txt: SiegeAbandonConfirmationHours, RoleMinimumHoldHours and
  UrgentDefenseLatchHours, with a "steadying the AI" section describing what to
  change when armies look indecisive.


Strategic Campaign AI - v0.3.0
==============================

Requires Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.4.8.


Changes
-------
- Improved kingdom target selection so armies commit to reachable frontline
  objectives instead of drifting between them.
- Rebalanced war goals, war exhaustion and peace pressure, so kingdoms take up
  distinct postures and contest border fiefs properly.
- Armies disengage from sieges they cannot win.
- Better garrison support for fiefs that are genuinely under threat.
- Improved raid response and defence of newly captured fiefs.
- Interception and avoidance behaviour reworked.
- Significant performance improvements to campaign AI scoring.
- Stability fixes.
- Strategic memory now persists across save and load.


Added
-----
- ModuleData/settings.txt - documented settings for tuning the AI, applied on
  restart. No rebuild needed.
- Optional diagnostic log for troubleshooting. Set VerboseLogging = true in
  settings.txt to write to Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\StrategicCampaignAI.log.


Notes
-----
Safe to add or remove mid-save.
Delete any previous version of the module folder before installing.
