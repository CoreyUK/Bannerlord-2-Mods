v1.1.0
- New "Stock arrows" option: two arrow barrels. Archers whose quivers drop below a threshold (refill_below in settings.txt) walk over and refill.
- New "Build an archer platform" option: a raised 12 x 8 m timber deck on posts with a full-width ramp, with its own walkable navmesh so formations can be ordered onto it.
- The platform navmesh piece is copied into Native/NavMeshPrefabs the first time a platform is built; the engine only reads that folder.
- Safety net around crew assignment so a machine without an AI object cannot end the battle.
- Optional debug=1 setting records game error stacks during a battle to debug.txt.

v1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Encounter menu options to buy barricades, a ballista and a catapult before a field battle (open-terrain battles only).
- Ghost previews and cards during deployment: click a card or press P, move with the mouse, rotate with the arrow keys, Enter to place.
- Barricades carry a blocker navmesh so the AI paths around them; horses that charge them are impaled.
- Ballista and catapult are crewed by your archers and fire on their own; a catapult left without a crew reloads itself.
- Prices, crew, ammo and keys are set in settings.txt.
